# Glassfin privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Glassfin is a player for Jellyfin servers that you run yourself. Its developer
collects no data about you: Glassfin has no accounts of its own, no analytics,
no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.

## What Glassfin connects to

- **Your Jellyfin server.** Glassfin signs in to the server address you enter
  and talks only to it for your library, playback, progress and downloads.
  What that server keeps is up to whoever runs it.
- **Wikidata**, to show awards on film and series pages. The phone asks
  Wikidata directly with the title's public identifiers. On by default; turn
  it off under Settings › Integrations.
- **OpenSubtitles**, only when you search for subtitles and your server has no
  subtitle provider. On by default; turn it off under Settings › Integrations.
- **Services you set up yourself**:
  - **Seerr**, at the address you enter, to request films and shows.
  - **Chromecast**, to play on a Cast device on your network.
  - Other servers you add under Settings › Integrations (Streamystats,
    Jellysearch, Marlin search).

## What stays on your phone

- Your server address and sign-in token, in Glassfin's own storage on the
  phone; accounts you choose to save, in the iOS Keychain.
- Your settings, and anything you download to watch offline.
- **Camera:** used only to scan the QR code your TV shows when you sign in
  there. No image is kept.
- **Location and Wi-Fi:** Glassfin reads the name of your Wi-Fi network, and
  keeps the home network names you choose in its settings, to use your
  server's home address while you're on it. Your location is never stored or
  sent anywhere.
- **Local network:** used to reach servers and Cast devices at home.

Deleting Glassfin removes all of this from your phone.

## Children

Glassfin isn't directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
